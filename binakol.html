Chicken Binakol

Overview

Chicken Binakol is a traditional dish from Antique known for its unique method of preparation and refreshing flavor. It is a type of chicken soup cooked using coconut water and sometimes prepared inside bamboo tubes or coconut shells.

The dish combines chicken meat with ingredients such as lemongrass, ginger, and young coconut meat, resulting in a flavorful and slightly sweet broth.

It reflects the resourcefulness of local communities in using natural materials and locally available ingredients.

Food Features

The dish is known for its light yet flavorful broth, which is enhanced by the natural sweetness of coconut water and meat.

Cooking the dish inside bamboo or coconut shells adds a subtle aroma that enhances the overall taste.

The combination of herbs and spices provides a balanced flavor that is both savory and refreshing.

Highlights

Coconut-Based Broth

Uses coconut water for a natural sweetness.

Traditional Cooking

Sometimes prepared using bamboo or coconut shells.

Fresh Ingredients

Includes herbs like ginger and lemongrass.

Distinct Flavor

A balance of savory and slightly sweet taste.

Things to Experience

  • Tasting the Dish – Experience its unique flavor.
  • Learning Preparation – Understand how it is cooked.
  • Food Photography – Capture its presentation.
  • Cultural Appreciation – Learn its local significance.

Availability

Chicken Binakol can be found in local eateries and during special occasions in Antique.

Visitors are encouraged to try the dish in local restaurants for an authentic experience.
