Banigan Festival

Overview

The Banigan Festival is celebrated in the municipality of Libertad, Antique. It highlights the town’s traditional weaving industry, particularly the production of banig or handwoven mats.

The festival serves as a way to promote local craftsmanship and preserve the weaving traditions passed down through generations.

It showcases the creativity and skill of local artisans while celebrating the cultural identity of the community.

Cultural Features

The Banigan Festival focuses on the traditional art of weaving banig, which are colorful mats made from natural materials.

The festival features displays of woven products, demonstrations of weaving techniques, and performances that incorporate the use of banig.

It reflects the importance of craftsmanship and cultural preservation in the community.

Highlights

Weaving Demonstrations

Shows the traditional process of making banig.

Colorful Displays

Exhibits of handcrafted woven products.

Cultural Performances

Dances inspired by weaving traditions.

Local Craft Promotion

Supports artisans and local industries.

Things to Experience

  • Watching Weaving – Observe traditional techniques.
  • Buying Crafts – Purchase handmade products.
  • Festival Viewing – Enjoy performances and events.
  • Photography – Capture colorful displays.
  • Cultural Appreciation – Learn about local traditions.

Travel Information

The Banigan Festival is held annually in March in Libertad, Antique.

Visitors can travel by land to reach the municipality and participate in festival activities.

It is recommended to visit early in the day to fully explore exhibits and performances.
