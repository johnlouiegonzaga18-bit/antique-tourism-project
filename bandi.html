Bandi

Overview

Bandi is a traditional sweet delicacy from Antique made from muscovado sugar and peanuts. It is commonly shaped into flat, round pieces and is known for its crunchy texture.

The dish reflects the importance of sugar production in the province and is often enjoyed as a snack or dessert.

Its simple ingredients and preparation highlight the creativity of local food traditions.

Food Features

Bandi is made by melting muscovado sugar until it caramelizes, then mixing it with roasted peanuts.

The mixture is shaped into round portions and left to cool, resulting in a firm and crunchy texture.

The use of muscovado sugar gives it a rich, deep sweetness that distinguishes it from other sweets.

Highlights

Traditional Snack

Popular among locals and visitors.

Things to Experience

  • Tasting Bandi – Enjoy its crunchy texture.
  • Buying Souvenirs – Take home local sweets.
  • Learning Preparation – Understand how it is made.
  • Food Appreciation – Explore traditional flavors.

Availability

Bandi is widely available in local markets and souvenir shops in Antique.

It is commonly sold as a packaged snack, making it easy for visitors to bring home.
