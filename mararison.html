Mararison Island (Culasi)

Overview

Mararison Island, located in the municipality of Culasi in Antique, is one of the province’s most well-known and frequently visited tourist destinations. The island is recognized for its pristine white sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, and peaceful natural environment that attracts both local and foreign visitors.

Unlike highly commercialized beach destinations, Mararison Island has maintained much of its natural charm. The island offers a relaxing atmosphere where visitors can enjoy scenic coastal views, fresh sea breeze, and a slower pace of life.

Its unique combination of coastal beauty and rolling hills makes it an ideal destination for those seeking both relaxation and light outdoor adventure.

Natural Features

Mararison Island is known for its diverse natural features that combine coastal and upland landscapes. The island’s white sand beaches stretch along clear turquoise waters, making it ideal for swimming and relaxation.

Beyond the coast, the island is also characterized by rolling green hills that provide panoramic views of the surrounding sea and neighboring islands. These elevated areas are popular among visitors for sightseeing and photography.

The surrounding waters are rich in marine life, making the island suitable for snorkeling and other water-based activities.

Things to Do

  • Swimming – Enjoy the clear and refreshing waters surrounding the island.
  • Snorkeling – Explore marine life in shallow coastal areas.
  • Island Hopping – Visit nearby beaches and sandbars.
  • Hiking – Walk up the hills for scenic viewpoints.
  • Photography – Capture landscapes, sunsets, and natural scenery.
  • Relaxation – Enjoy the calm environment and sea breeze.

Travel Information

Mararison Island can be accessed via a short boat ride from Culasi, Antique. Travel arrangements are usually coordinated through local tourism offices or boat operators in the area.

The best time to visit is during the dry season, from November to May, when sea conditions are calm and suitable for outdoor activities.

Visitors are encouraged to bring essential items such as drinking water, sun protection, and personal supplies, as facilities on the island are limited.
