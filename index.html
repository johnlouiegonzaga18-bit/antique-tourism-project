About Antique

Antique is a peaceful province in the western part of Panay Island, known for its unspoiled natural beauty and rich cultural heritage. From pristine beaches and hidden islands to cool mountain landscapes, Antique offers a quiet escape away from crowded tourist destinations. Visitors can experience vibrant festivals, historic landmarks, and traditional crafts that reflect the province’s unique identity.
