Craftsmanship of Antique

Antique is known for its rich tradition of craftsmanship, where skills have been passed down from generation to generation. These crafts reflect the creativity, resourcefulness, and cultural identity of the local communities.

From weaving colorful textiles to producing durable earthenware, these traditional practices continue to play an important role in the province’s culture and economy.

Visitors can explore these crafts through local centers and workshops, gaining a deeper appreciation of Antique’s artistic heritage.
