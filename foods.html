Flavors of Antique

Antique offers a variety of traditional dishes that reflect its culture, resources, and way of life. The local cuisine is known for its simple preparation, natural ingredients, and distinct flavors.

Many dishes are prepared using locally available materials such as coconut, fresh seafood, and agricultural products. These ingredients contribute to the freshness and authenticity of the food.

Exploring Antique’s cuisine allows visitors to experience the province not only through its sights but also through its taste.
