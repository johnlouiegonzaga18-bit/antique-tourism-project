Tibiao

Overview

Tibiao, located in the province of Antique, is widely known as the adventure capital of the region. Surrounded by lush forests, rivers, and mountains, it offers a wide range of outdoor activities that attract thrill-seekers and nature lovers.

The town is famous for its unique kawa hot bath experience, where visitors relax in large cooking pots filled with warm water. In addition to this, Tibiao provides various water-based and land-based activities that highlight its natural environment.

Its combination of adventure and relaxation makes Tibiao a popular destination for visitors looking for both excitement and a connection with nature.

Natural Features

Tibiao is characterized by its rich natural surroundings, including rivers, forests, and mountainous landscapes. Its clean and flowing rivers are among its most important features, supporting both tourism and local activities.

The area is covered with dense vegetation, providing a refreshing environment and scenic views. These natural features make Tibiao ideal for outdoor recreation and eco-tourism.

The cool and natural setting enhances the overall experience, allowing visitors to enjoy both adventure and relaxation in one destination.

Things to Do

  • Kawa Hot Bath – Relax in heated water inside large cooking pots.
  • White Water Kayaking – Navigate through flowing river currents.
  • River Tubing – Float along the river while enjoying the scenery.
  • Fish Spa – Experience natural foot spa in the river.
  • Nature Exploration – Walk and explore surrounding forests.

Travel Information

Tibiao can be reached by land transportation within Antique. Local guides and tour operators are available to assist visitors with activities and safety.

The best time to visit is during the dry season, when river conditions are safer and more suitable for outdoor activities.

Visitors are encouraged to wear comfortable clothing and bring extra clothes, as most activities involve water.
