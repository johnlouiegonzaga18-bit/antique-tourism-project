San Juan Nepomuceno Parish Church (Anini-y Church)

Overview

The San Juan Nepomuceno Parish Church, located in Anini-y, Antique, is the only fully preserved Spanish colonial church in the province. It stands as a significant symbol of Antique’s religious and historical heritage.

Constructed between 1879 and 1898, the church has remained intact through time and continues to serve as an active place of worship.

Its well-preserved structure makes it an important landmark for both religious and historical appreciation.

Architectural Features

The church is built using coral stones and lime, materials commonly used during the Spanish colonial period.

Its design reflects 19th-century Baroque architecture, characterized by thick walls and a solid structure.

The durability of its construction has allowed it to remain well-preserved over the years.

Things to Experience

  • Visiting the Church – Explore its structure and design.
  • Attending Mass – Participate in religious services.
  • Photography – Capture architectural details.
  • Historical Learning – Understand its significance.

Travel Information

The San Juan Nepomuceno Parish Church is located in the municipality of Anini-y, the southernmost town of Antique. It can be reached through land transportation via major roads connecting different towns in the province.

The church is accessible to visitors throughout the year and is often included in heritage and pilgrimage tours within Antique.

Visitors are advised to dress appropriately and observe silence and respect, especially during religious services. Photography may be allowed, but it is important to be mindful of ongoing activities inside the church.
