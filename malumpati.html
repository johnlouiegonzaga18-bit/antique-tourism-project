Malumpati Cold Spring (Pandan)

Overview

Malumpati Cold Spring, located in Pandan, Antique, is one of the most popular natural attractions in the province. It is known for its clear, cold, and refreshing river that provides a relaxing environment for visitors.

Surrounded by lush greenery, the spring offers a peaceful setting ideal for families and groups. Its clean waters and natural surroundings make it a perfect place for swimming and recreation.

The destination is well-loved for its accessibility and its ability to provide a refreshing escape from the heat.

Natural Features

Malumpati Cold Spring is known for its crystal-clear waters that flow from natural underground sources. The river remains cool even during hot weather, making it a refreshing place for visitors.

The area is surrounded by trees and vegetation, creating a shaded and relaxing environment. The natural setting enhances the overall experience and provides a calm atmosphere.

The river’s gentle current makes it suitable for swimming and other recreational activities.

Relaxing Environment

Perfect for family outings and leisure activities.

  • Swimming – Enjoy the cool and refreshing waters of the spring.
  • Bamboo Rafting – Ride along the river while enjoying the scenery.
  • River Tubing – Float along the gentle current of the river.
  • Picnicking – Spend time with family and friends in a natural setting.
  • Relaxation – Enjoy the calm and shaded riverside environment.

Travel Information

Malumpati Cold Spring is accessible by land travel and is one of the most visited natural attractions in Antique.

The best time to visit is during weekdays or early in the morning to avoid large crowds, especially during peak seasons.

Visitors are advised to follow safety guidelines, wear appropriate swimwear, and help maintain cleanliness to preserve the natural environment.
